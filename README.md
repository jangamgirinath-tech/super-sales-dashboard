# superstore-sales-dashboard

The Super Store Sales Power BI dashboard turns raw retail transaction data into clear, interactive visuals across two report pages. It helps you quickly see how sales, profit, and customer behavior vary by product category, shipping mode, and discount level — so you can make smarter business decisions.

1. Project Title / Headline

Super Store Sales Data Analytics Dashboard

2. Short Description / Purpose

This dashboard analyzes a super store's sales performance across product categories, sub-categories, customers, and shipping modes to uncover profitability trends and improve business decisions. It helps stakeholders quickly identify top-performing product lines, high-value customers, and the impact of discounting on profit.

3. Tech Stack (Power BI)


Power BI Desktop – Data visualization & multi-page dashboard creation
Power Query – Data cleaning & transformation (e.g., handling nulls, formatting date/currency columns)
DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Measures such as Sum of Sales, Sum of Profit, Profit Margin %, and Average Order Value
Data Modeling – Relationships between orders, products, and customer tables
Decomposition Tree & Gauge visuals – For interactive profit breakdown and performance-vs-target tracking


4. Data Source


Dataset: Super Store Sales Dataset (classic retail orders dataset covering Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology categories)
File: Excel / CSV order-level transaction data

5. Features / Highlights

📊 KPI Cards (Quick Insights)
Example: Sum of Sales = $2M, Sum of Profit = $286K, Sum of Quantity = 38K, Profit Margin = 12.47%, Average Order Value = 458.61 → Instantly shows overall performance.

📈 Sales Trends by Quarter
Example: Sales dip from $0.51M in Qtr 1 to $0.46M in Qtr 2, then recover strongly to $0.62M in Qtr 3 and $0.70M in Qtr 4 → Highlights seasonal momentum building through the second half of the year.

🌳 Profit Contribution Analysis (Decomposition Tree)
Example: Of total profit of $2,86,397, Technology contributes $1,45,455, Office Supplies $1,22,491, and Furniture only $18,451 → Shows Furniture is dragging down overall profitability despite decent sales volume.

🏆 Top 10 Products by Revenue
Example: Canon imageCLASS leads with $62K in revenue, followed by Fellowes PB ($27K) and Cisco TelePresence ($23K) → Helps focus inventory and marketing on high-revenue SKUs.

💸 Impact of Discounts on Profitability
Example: Binders receive the heaviest discounting (~500 in discount sum) yet still post strong profit, while Copiers achieve high profit ($60K) with minimal discounting → Useful for identifying which categories can sustain discounts without hurting margin, and which are being over-discounted for little return.

👥 Top Customers by Profit
Example: Tamara Chand is the most profitable customer at $8,981, followed by Raymond Buch ($6,976) and Sanjit Chand ($5,757) → Enables targeted retention strategies for high-value customers.

🎯 Profit Performance vs Target Profit (Gauge)
Example: Actual profit of $286K against a target of $573K → Shows the business is running at roughly half of its profit goal, flagging a performance gap for leadership.

🚚 Category-wise Ship Distribution
Example: Standard Class dominates shipping at 59.72% (5.97K orders), while Same Day shipping is used in only 5.43% of orders → Useful for logistics planning and evaluating premium shipping demand.

📶 Profit Contribution by Category (Waterfall)
Example: Technology adds $0.15M, Office Supplies adds $0.12M, and Furniture adds just $0.02M to build up to the $0.29M total profit → Visually reinforces where profit is actually being generated.

📋 Detailed Transaction Table
Example: Customer-level view showing Quantity, Sum of Sales, and Sum of Profit, sortable by any metric → Enables drill-down analysis into individual customer performance.

🔍 Interactive Filters (Slicers)
Example: Filter by Category and Region, Ship Mode, or Order Date → Makes the dashboard dynamic, letting users isolate performance for a specific segment or time period.
